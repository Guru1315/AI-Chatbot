SAVEETHA UNIVERSITY (SIMATS ENGINEERING)
=========================================


1. COLLEGE NAME
---------------
Official name : Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences (SIMATS)
Also known as : Saveetha University
                SIMATS Engineering
                SIMATS University
                Saveetha Engineering College


2. WHAT MAKES OUR COLLEGE SPECIAL
---------------------------------
- Incredible architecture: a large, modern campus with impressive buildings.
- Flexible learning methods: students get freedom in how they learn.
- Finland study system: our college follows the Finnish approach to
  education, which sets it apart from most other colleges.


3. INTERESTING FACTS AND INFORMATION
------------------------------------

History
- It began in 1988 as Saveetha Dental College.
- It was granted deemed university status in 2005 and became the Saveetha
  Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences.
- It was known as SIMATS Deemed University and was renamed Saveetha
  University in 2016.
- The founder is Dr. N.M. Veeraiyan, a well-known educator and dentist.
- The university motto is "Knowledge Conquers All".
- Saveetha Engineering College started in 2006.

Location and Campus
- Located in Saveetha Nagar, Thandalam, near Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
- The campus spreads over 180+ acres, with about 70 lakh sq. ft. of built
  area.
- It has around 13 institutions, roughly 5,000 staff and about 10,000
  students.

Accreditation and Approvals
- NAAC A++ grade (CGPA 3.66 out of 4).
- Recognised by UGC and approved by AICTE and the National Medical
  Commission.
- SIMATS Engineering has NBA Tier 1 accreditation for its CSE, ECE, IT and
  Biomedical Engineering programs.
- SIMATS Engineering has IET UK accreditation for its CSE, ECE, Mechanical
  and EEE programs.
- SIMATS Engineering is ISO 9001:2015 certified.
- SIMATS Engineering has received a Platinum rating from QS I-GAUGE.

Rankings
- NIRF 2025: 13th in the University category and 24th overall.
- NIRF 2025: Saveetha Dental College is 2nd in Dental, and Saveetha School
  of Law is 19th in Law.
- Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2025: 401-500 band.
- Globally ranked 3rd for SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) in the Times
  Higher Education Impact Rankings.
- Saveetha Dental College is ranked 26th worldwide in dentistry in the QS
  World University Rankings by Subject 2026.
- SIMATS Engineering states on its website that it holds 53rd position in
  the NIRF Engineering category.

Institutions under SIMATS
- Dental College, Medical College and Hospital, Engineering, Law,
  Management, Pharmacy, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy,
  Allied Health Sciences, Architecture and Design, and Liberal Arts and
  Sciences.
- It is one of the private universities in India with full academic
  autonomy.

Placements (as reported by education portals for 2025)
- Highest package: about 15 LPA.
- Average package: about 9 LPA.
- A dedicated training and placement cell helps with mock interviews and
  resume building.

Popular Programs
- B.Tech, MBBS, BDS, B.Arch, MBA, B.Sc, BBA LLB (Hons), M.Tech and more.
